If you got most of these

Then you have the thing this site was built to give you, which is not a list of architectures. It is the habit of asking what kind of feedback a problem provides, how much of it there is, and what a failure looks like before you have a name for it. Everything else — the specific layer types, the current best optimiser, whichever architecture is fashionable this year — is detail you can look up.

Where to go from here

The code on this site is deliberately small and readable, and the most useful next step is to change it rather than read more of it. Some suggestions that are genuinely within reach:

  • Add a second attention head to assets/js/lib/vit.js and see whether the attention maps specialise.
  • Give the Rocket League cars a boost action — a fourth output and a fuel counter in the observation — and watch how the reward shaping has to change with it.
  • Replace the racer's mutation with crossover between two parents, and measure whether it actually helps on the Grand tour.
  • Swap the CNN's max pooling for average pooling and explain the accuracy difference from what you know about what pooling throws away.
  • Add a fifth dataset to the foundations playground that no architecture on the page can solve, and work out why.
